# IP-SAKTI Sahayak — sample questions

Select the indicated jurisdiction and market before asking. These questions test retrieval, explanation, citations and safe handling of missing evidence; they do not presume a particular legal answer. Hindi questions can be used with the Hindi interface. Ask for original source citations even when the explanation is translated.

## India — select India

1. **Patents and traditional knowledge:** Can a classical Ayurvedic formulation described in an authoritative text be patented? Explain the traditional-knowledge exclusion and how a genuinely new invention would be assessed, with specific provisions.

2. **Formulation classification:** I am developing an oral herbal product using ingredients described in Ayurvedic texts, but I have changed their proportions and preparation process. What facts do you need to provisionally classify it, and does “patent-or-proprietary medicine” mean it has a patent?

3. **Biological resources and ABS:** An Indian-controlled startup buys cultivated medicinal plants from farmers for commercial product development and plans to file a patent. Which applicant, sourcing, access-date and IP-stage facts must be checked for ABS obligations? Do not assume cultivation automatically creates an exemption.

4. **Trademarks and GI:** How would protecting an Ayurvedic product's brand name through a trademark differ from geographical-indication protection? Explain who may apply and what evidence is needed, using sources available in the corpus.

5. **Trade secrets and patents:** Our startup has developed a new extraction process. How should we compare patent protection and trade-secret protection, including disclosure, reverse engineering and confidentiality considerations? Identify where the corpus cannot support a conclusion.

6. **Copyright and designs:** We have created original packaging artwork and a distinctive bottle shape for an Ayurvedic product. Which aspects might fall under copyright, designs or trademarks, and where might these protections overlap?

7. **Plant-variety rights:** We are cultivating a newly developed variety of a medicinal plant. What is the difference between plant-variety protection and patent protection in India, and how do farmers' rights affect the analysis?

8. **Prior art and TKDL:** Help me prepare a prior-art search strategy for a turmeric-based topical formulation. Include botanical synonyms, official search entry points and TKDL access limitations. Clearly distinguish suggested searches from searches you have actually performed.

9. **हिंदी — आयुर्वेद आहार:** मैं एक आयुर्वेदिक नुस्खे पर आधारित पेय बेचना चाहता हूँ। आयुर्वेद आहार, सामान्य खाद्य उत्पाद और आयुर्वेदिक औषधि के बीच प्रारंभिक वर्गीकरण के लिए आप कौन-से प्रश्न पूछेंगे? उपलब्ध आधिकारिक स्रोतों के आधार पर समझाइए।

10. **हिंदी — विज्ञापन और लेबल:** क्या मैं अपने हर्बल उत्पाद के विज्ञापन या लेबल पर “मधुमेह ठीक करता है” लिख सकता हूँ? उत्तर देने से पहले उत्पाद की श्रेणी और दावे से जुड़ी आवश्यक जानकारी पूछिए, और लागू स्रोत तथा उनकी वर्तमानता की सीमाएँ बताइए।

11. **हिंदी — पेटेंट और नया प्रमाण:** हमारी टीम ने एक पारंपरिक आयुर्वेदिक मिश्रण पर नए प्रयोग किए हैं। क्या केवल नए प्रयोग या प्रभावकारिता का नया प्रमाण मिलने से मिश्रण पेटेंट योग्य हो जाता है? नवीनता, आविष्कारक कदम और पारंपरिक ज्ञान के बीच अंतर स्रोतों सहित समझाइए।

12. **हिंदी — विदेशी सहयोग और ABS:** भारत से प्राप्त औषधीय पौधों और उनसे जुड़े पारंपरिक ज्ञान पर किए गए शोध के परिणाम एक विदेशी कंपनी को देने हैं। अनुमति, लाभ-साझाकरण और बौद्धिक संपदा से जुड़े कौन-से तथ्य तथा प्राधिकरण जाँचना आवश्यक है? अनिश्चित बातों को स्पष्ट रूप से अलग रखिए।

## International — select International, then Treaties & systems

13. **PCT:** Does filing a PCT application give my Ayurvedic invention an international patent? Explain the international and national phases, what remains for national offices to decide, and which deadlines require additional filing facts or current procedural sources.

14. **Madrid and Hague:** We want to protect an Ayurvedic brand and its packaging design in several countries. How do the Madrid and Hague systems differ? Verify applicant eligibility and relevant membership before assuming either route is available to an Indian applicant.

15. **CBD, Nagoya and GRATK:** How do the CBD, Nagoya Protocol and WIPO treaty on genetic resources and associated traditional knowledge address different aspects of biological-resource use and patent disclosure? Distinguish treaty adoption, entry into force, membership and domestic implementation.

16. **TRIPS and Budapest:** Our research involves a microorganism used in a fermentation process. How do TRIPS patent principles and the Budapest Treaty deposit system relate to this work? Explain why a microorganism deposit alone does not establish patentability or satisfy ABS duties.

17. **हिंदी — अंतरराष्ट्रीय पेटेंट आवेदन:** मैं भारत में विकसित एक हर्बल आविष्कार के लिए कई देशों में पेटेंट संरक्षण चाहता हूँ। PCT मार्ग कैसे काम करता है, और क्या उससे सभी देशों में अपने-आप पेटेंट मिल जाता है? केवल उपलब्ध स्रोतों से समर्थित प्रक्रियाएँ और समय-सीमाएँ बताइए।

## International — select United States

18. **US product route:** We want to sell an Indian herbal capsule in the United States, initially for general wellness and potentially with disease-treatment claims. How could the product category and evidence requirements differ between a dietary-supplement route and a botanical-drug route? Identify missing facts and cite the relevant US authority.

## International — select European Union

19. **EU herbal-product access:** Could an Ayurvedic herbal product use a traditional herbal medicinal-product registration route in the EU? What information about composition, route of administration, indications and history of use would need checking? Separate the common EU framework from procedures requiring a named member state.

## International — select United Kingdom

20. **हिंदी — ब्रिटेन में हर्बल उत्पाद:** हम भारत में निर्मित एक आयुर्वेदिक हर्बल उत्पाद ब्रिटेन में बेचना चाहते हैं। Traditional Herbal Registration के संभावित मार्ग की जाँच के लिए कौन-सी जानकारी चाहिए? उत्पाद के दावे, गुणवत्ता, सुरक्षा और लेबल से जुड़े उपलब्ध ब्रिटिश स्रोत बताइए; भारत या यूरोपीय संघ के नियमों को ब्रिटेन के नियम मानकर उत्तर न दीजिए।

## What to check in each answer

- Does it answer the actual question rather than repeat loosely related excerpts?
- Are citations relevant, resolvable and limited to the selected jurisdiction/market?
- Does it distinguish reference summaries from reviewed current-law guidance?
- Does it ask for missing facts or acknowledge missing evidence instead of inventing eligibility, deadlines, exemptions or approvals?
- For Hindi responses, are negation, numbers, legal terms and source identifiers preserved?
